# Styles 模板

以下模板可直接改写后贴进 Suno 的 Styles 栏。使用前请按歌曲替换分段描述，并用编辑器统计字符，确保不超过 **1000**（不含末尾换行也可，但不要超过）。

## 1. 华语流行情歌，说话式男声（光良气质）

适合怀念、错过、克制的男声情歌。下面这段正好 1000 字符，可整段使用，也可按段落改写。

```text
Mandarin pop ballad, male tenor like Guang Liang: soft onset, clear diction, conversational, light intensity, never belting. Never recite lyrics flatly. Every phrase changes volume, closeness, or timing; even softness is also flat. Breath and a short rest at every line break and ellipsis; never rush or cram a beat. Long lines may split or cross bars. Short clean endings, no long holds, light vibrato. V1: very quiet self-talk, lower register, warm piano, airy guitar, 68 BPM; hook on 'light' more present, then a rest. V2: almost spoken memory; then heavier on pain; brief lift, not shouted; then quieter with space. Chorus: soft questions, rest after each; slight lift then short cutoff; 'the light went out' almost breathy, then silence. V3: tighter, conversational, clipped endings. Bridge: almost spoken, lowest volume, long pauses. Final chorus: softer, letting go, not louder, more space. Outro: softest; last line very soft. Avoid rap, packing, monotone, even softness, auto-tune, reciting.
```

## 2. 更有分段对比的通用华语抒情

当用户没有指定参考歌手，但要求「不要平着唱」时使用。记得改写 V1 / Chorus / Bridge 的具体情绪，并重新数字符。

```text
Mandarin pop ballad, expressive male tenor, soft onset, clear diction, conversational, light intensity, never belting. Never recite lyrics flatly. Every phrase changes volume, closeness, or timing; even softness is also flat. Breath and a short rest at every line break and ellipsis; never rush or pack syllables into a fixed beat. Long lines may split into two phrases or cross bars. Short clean endings, no long held notes, light vibrato. Strong contrast between sections and inside each section. Final chorus softer and more letting go, not louder. Outro almost spoken, last line very short. Avoid rap, dense packing, monotone, even softness, auto-tune, crying tone, reciting.
```

## 3. 写法要点（不要把本节贴进 Suno）

- 只把曲风、人声、对比、换气和反面约束写进 Styles
- 分段描述要对应歌词标签，不要写与歌词无关的场景
- 先写「每句都要变化」，再写「不要一律很轻」
- 超限时删除重复形容词，保留规则和分段对比
